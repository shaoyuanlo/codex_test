About

I am an Assistant Professor at National Taiwan University. My research spans trustworthy AI, machine learning, and multimodal models, with a recent focus on multimodal large language models and reasoning.

Previously, I was a Research Scientist at Honda Research Institute USA. I received my Ph.D. from Johns Hopkins University in 2023, and my M.S. and B.S. from National Chiao Tung University in 2019 and 2017. I also spent two wonderful summers with Amazon's Just Walk Out and Astro teams.
